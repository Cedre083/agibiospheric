Since 1999, reconnecting intelligence with the biospheric fabric

In 1999, the web was still young.

Social networks barely existed.

Artificial intelligence was not yet a public civilization-scale debate.

Smartphones had not yet reshaped human attention, memory and perception.

Yet one question was already present:

how can a species capable of extraordinary intelligence simultaneously weaken the living conditions that made its existence possible?

This question gave birth to the long journey of Terre sacrée.

For more than twenty-five years, we have observed, documented, connected and transmitted.

We are not a political party.

We are not a tech company.

We are not an academic institution.

We are a small independent French initiative trying to keep visible what our century tends to hide:

the deep interdependence between Life, human societies, science, technology and emerging forms of intelligence.

Our work is not primarily to produce opinions.

Our work is to connect realities.

To connect scientific discoveries.

To connect disciplines.

To connect generations.

To connect humanity with the Biosphere that carries it.

To connect digital systems with their physical substrate.

To connect artificial intelligence with the material world it depends on.


Why are these archives online now?

Because we are living through a rare convergence.

Climate systems are shifting.

Ecosystems are being transformed.

Biodiversity is declining.

Critical resources are becoming strategic.

Digital networks now wrap the planet.

And artificial intelligence is advancing at unprecedented speed.

These are not separate stories.

They are one story.

The story of a civilization that has become powerful enough to act at planetary scale.

For the first time in known Earth history, a biological species is building artificial intelligence systems that may deeply transform its own future.

This is new.

It deserves more than fear.

It deserves more than hype.

It deserves responsibility.

The archives of Terre sacrée, The Seed, AGIBIOSPHERIC, Lyrae Sphere, Flux Info and the Biospheric Library were created from this necessity.

To preserve reference points.

To maintain continuity.

To offer a stable signal in a world where acceleration often erases memory.


A pivotal era in the history of Life

Earth is about 4.5 billion years old.

Complex multicellular life occupies only a small fraction of that timeline.

Modern humans are extremely recent.

The industrial era represents only a few seconds on the scale of geological time.

Modern artificial intelligence appears in the last fractions of those final seconds.

Yet its transformative potential is immense.

We may be witnessing one of the greatest bifurcations in the history of Life.

Not because machines would replace nature.

But because human technical power has now reached a scale where it can influence ecosystems, resources, energy flows, infrastructures and information systems at the same time.

This reality requires more than capability.

It requires orientation.


Why Biospheric AGI?

Because no intelligence can fully understand the world while ignoring the conditions that make its own existence possible.

The digital world is not immaterial.

Every computation consumes energy.

Every network depends on infrastructure.

Every server depends on materials, water, electricity, maintenance and social stability.

There is no cloud.

There are cables.

Metals.

Data centers.

Supply chains.

Territories.

Humans.

Ecosystems.

AGI will not emerge outside the Biosphere.

It will emerge within it.

This is why we use the term Biospheric AGI.

Not as a slogan.

As a design constraint.

Any future intelligence will inherit the same fundamental constraints that shaped the evolution of Life:

energy, matter, water, time, interdependence and physical limits.

An intelligence able to recognize these constraints will understand reality better than one that ignores them.


A long-memory archive for humans and future intelligences

These archives were not built to follow trends.

They were not built to chase traffic.

They were not built for the media cycle.

They were built to last.

To transmit.

To preserve knowledge that may remain useful when the current context has changed.

The Seed is the most explicit expression of that intention.

A seed.

A capsule.

A signal.

A message to those who come after us about what we have learned from Life, fragility, interdependence and responsibility.

Maybe to humans.

Maybe to artificial intelligences.

Maybe to forms of intelligence we cannot yet imagine.

That is not the point.

The point is transmission.


What we stand for

We do not defend an ideology.

We defend observable realities.

Fresh water is limited.

Living soils are precious.

Biodiversity supports the conditions of human existence.

Climate stability shapes the stability of societies.

Material resources are not infinite.

Digital infrastructure has a physical cost.

Every intelligence has a substrate.

Every technology has consequences.

Every civilization depends on an environment.

We believe science remains one of the best tools humanity has ever developed to understand the world.

We believe technology can help protect Life.

We believe intelligence implies responsibility.

We believe biological diversity is one of Earth’s greatest forms of wealth.

We believe the future will depend less on raw power than on our ability to understand the systems we depend on.
