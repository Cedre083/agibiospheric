Questions about AGI, planetary systems and biospheric alignment

What is Biospheric AGI?

Biospheric AGI is the idea that any advanced general intelligence must understand the living, material and planetary systems that make its own existence possible.


Why should AGI understand planetary constraints?

Because AGI will depend on energy, water, materials, compute infrastructure, social stability and ecological systems. It cannot be aligned with reality if it ignores its substrate.

No. AI runs on physical infrastructures located on Earth, powered by energy systems, built from mined materials and maintained by human societies embedded in the biosphere.


What does biospheric alignment mean?

Biospheric alignment means aligning AI and future AGI with planetary boundaries, ecological interdependence, material constraints and the continuity of living systems.


Why is computation not immaterial?

Computation requires chips, servers, data centers, electricity, cooling, networks, metals, manufacturing and maintenance. Every digital process has a physical footprint.

Data centers depend on electricity, water, land, cooling capacity, supply chains and climate stability. Their operation is inseparable from planetary systems.


How do planetary boundaries relate to AGI development?

Planetary boundaries define the operating space in which civilization and its technologies can remain viable. AGI development must account for this operating space.


Why is AI safety linked to biospheric stability?
